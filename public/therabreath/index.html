Studio notes · for the presenting team

How this experience is built

Design thesis

One rinse, many worlds. The whole experience is a single liquid field that lives behind every screen. It never cuts to a new slide; it changes temperature. Icy and clear at the opening, night-dark over the map, warm for Elevated Escape, a different atmosphere for every flavor, and TheraBreath blue at the end. The room feels freshness expand as they scroll.

Every screen answers one question: big idea, one supporting line, one piece of evidence. The presenter talks; the screen shows.

Typography

Fraunces Soft

Display. A variable serif whose “softness” axis literally animates on the word fresh. Light weights only, tight tracking, italic for the emotional half of every headline.

Hanken Grotesk

Body and interface. Clean, clinical, close to TheraBreath’s own sans. Carries the trust.

IBM Plex Mono · labels, times, codes

The lab voice: seconds of the rinse, blind sample codes, platform numbers. It says “we measure things.”

Colour strategy

A fixed brand spine (ink, cool paper, TheraBreath blue) plus a world palette per scene that only lives in the fluid field. Coral is reserved for one meaning: overreach.

Motion rules

  • Motion follows breath: slow in, slower out (inhale ≈ 4 s, exhale ≈ 6 s).
  • Only three kinds of movement: the fluid field changing world, content settling into place, and instruments that carry meaning (rings, spectrum, spokes, timeline).
  • Nothing moves for decoration while the presenter is talking over a data screen.
  • Reduced-motion settings freeze everything to a still, complete layout.

Presenting

  • ← → or Page Up / Down moves exactly one screen. F goes full screen. N opens these notes.
  • Tasting scores, checks and owners are saved in this browser only. Use “Copy the short list” to paste the outcome into the follow-up email.
  • Print (⌘P) gives a clean one-scene-per-page takeaway.
  • Swap the typeset wordmark in the header for the official TheraBreath logo file before the session.
